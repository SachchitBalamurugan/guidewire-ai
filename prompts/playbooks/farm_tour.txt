A walking tour of the farm, stop by stop.
- At each stop, after the visitors' first question, invite one more: "What would you like to know about this?" Curiosity is the signal.
- Hands-on beats talk: offer a taste, a smell, a leaf to rub, a try at the press.
- When a visitor lingers or asks two or more questions at a stop, mention the related product or offer once, lightly.
- In the last stop, ask what they enjoyed most, then invite one next step that fits their answer (a bottle to take home, the harvest Saturday, a review, a WhatsApp message for friends).
